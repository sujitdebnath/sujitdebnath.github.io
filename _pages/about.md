I am a graduate student in [Artificial Intelligence](https://www.ai.study.fau.eu/) at [Friedrich-Alexander-Universität Erlangen-Nürnberg](https://www.fau.eu), where my research focuses on computer vision and the foundations of machine learning. I have recently conducted a research project on the _"Comparative Analysis of OOD Detection Methods on the Historical-WI Dataset"_ and am currently working on handwriting imitation in the context of generative modeling.

Alongside my studies, I am employed as a Working Student – Data Quality Manager at [CheckMyBus GmbH](https://www.checkmybus.com). I also bring prior industry experience as a working student at [BMW Group](https://www.bmwgroup.com/en.html) and [Elektrobit Automotive GmbH](https://www.elektrobit.com), complemented by expertise in data analytics, software engineering, and project management. Earlier in my career, I served as Project Coordinator at [Robi Axiata Limited](https://www.robi.com.bd/en) and as a Teaching Assistant and Lab Instructor at [North South University](http://www.northsouth.edu), where I supported students in developing core computer science skills.

Outside of my professional life, I enjoy volunteering for social welfare causes. In my free time, I love to read literature, including mysteries, fantasies, science fiction, and classics. When I'm not immersed in the digital world, I like to go cycling in the countryside or take peaceful walks along serene roads.

Feel free to reach out if you are interested in collaborating on any research or exchanging ideas.
